# Assignment 4, Part 1 — Market Workstation

**Completed work.** Built a local Vue frontend, FastAPI backend, and SQLite database. Yahoo Finance observations stream through yfinance into the backend, are saved to SQLite, and reach the browser through a WebSocket. The dashboard displays updating one-minute candlesticks and observed volume for AAPL, MSFT, SPY, and BTC-USD. It includes a searchable watchlist, source age, source and receipt timestamps, feed/browser connection status, provisional-candle labels, and replay of stored observations.

**Key design decisions.** TradingView Lightweight Charts provides candlestick and volume panes. Source timestamps determine candle intervals; receipt timestamps preserve when observations became available locally. Exact duplicate payloads are ignored, while changed observations with the same symbol and source timestamp append revisions. Late observations rebuild affected bars. Replay advances in arrival order and excludes revisions that had not yet arrived. The current minute remains provisional until its interval ends. The interface uses recorded-data summaries and labels changes as recorded-period changes, rather than presenting them as daily returns. See the [design notes and SQLite schema](https://github.com/kuanenlee0925-blip/market-workstation/blob/main/docs/design.md).

**Remaining limitations.** Streaming quotes are sampled observations, so candles are not authoritative exchange OHLCV. Volume uses increases in the cumulative source total and can be incomplete or distorted by feed gaps. Symbol plus source timestamp is a heuristic observation identity. Ended candles may still change after late observations arrive. Source availability and freshness are not guaranteed; a connected socket does not establish fresh data. An actual provider outage/recovery was not induced: the unavailable-feed browser check uses a mock. Recording is currently unbounded and full snapshots are intended for a small local demonstration. This Part 1 submission does not claim the indicators, trading simulation, backtesting, or AI integrations required in later parts.
