# Assignment 4, Part 2 — Indicators, Simulated Trading and Backtesting

The Part 2 application and evidence were reviewed by the student and published to the public repository. The application version is identified below; a documentation follow-up records the submitted report and publication checks.

**Completed.** The localhost Vue/FastAPI/SQLite workstation displays two configurable simple moving averages and confirmed crossover arrows. A long-only simulated account applies the SMA crossover strategy, tracks pending/rejected/filled orders, fills, cash, shares, weighted-average cost, realized/unrealized P&L and equity history. Historical backtests evaluate recorded arrival prefixes and retain separate accounts and input snapshots. The terminal design, watchlist, chart/sidebar, Yahoo streaming and recorded replay are retained. All trading is simulated. The AI assistant is outside this part's requirements; API credits are not needed for Part 2.

**Design.** Only completed candles enter the SMAs; warm-up and unfinished values remain unavailable. Ties retain the previous side. Up crossover buys a fixed whole-share quantity when flat; down crossover closes. An accepted order fills at the next eligible observed price after its submission/decision, never at the observation that first reveals its own signal. Fees, spread and slippage are zero. Cash and shares are rechecked at fill, and accounting commits atomically. Strategy evaluations use only observations/corrections already received. Existing decision/fill/P&L evidence is not rewritten by later revisions. Detailed rules, schema, timing and limitations are in [Part 2 design](https://github.com/kuanenlee0925-blip/market-workstation/blob/07756dab80de868227d22efbec2780c60651c1fa/docs/part2-design.md).

**Limitations.** Sparse quote-derived bars are not a complete trade tape. Time-complete candles can have incomplete minute coverage. Zero costs and ideal full fills can overstate outcomes. Drawdown is sampled at ledger valuation events. The historical example is short, single-instrument and subject to selection/survivorship bias and parameter overfitting. No out-of-sample profitability, benchmark edge or generalization is established. Recorded backtests are limited to 3,000 arrivals; large-scale performance is unverified. The demo's disposable database was removed; the recorded summary/fills and video remain, while normal runs preserve exact inputs in the ignored local SQLite database. The video does not demonstrate every manual-account rejection/restart case; those have separate automated browser/database evidence. AI calls remain unavailable due to API credit balance and are not required here.
